## Finding 1 — MEDIUM–HIGH: the evidence report can name a strategy that did not run

- **Where:** `profiles/hylogger/generation/generate.py:379-381` and `profiles/hylogger/capabilities.py:354-357` (the `generate` step).
- **What happens:**
  - `generate()` defaults a missing `plan["strategy"]` to `fitted_markov_joint`.
  - The `generate` step passes only `ctx.plan()`. Nothing in `engine/` copies the spec's strategy into the plan.
  - `plan.validate_plan` (`profiles/hylogger/generation/plan.py:92,105-116`) accepts a plan with no `strategy`, and its docstring says that plan "runs under the run spec's own strategy".
  - `engine/core/report.py:109` prints `plan.get('strategy', spec.get('strategy'))`.
- **Failure scenario:**
  - The spec says `strategy: fitted_markov_balanced`, and `ruloom run --plan` replays a saved plan `{"seed": …, "n_rows": …}` with no strategy key. A plan saved before this feature, or a hand-edited one, would look like this.
  - The replay path in `capabilities.plan` only validates the plan, so it passes.
  - The generator runs `fitted_markov_joint`, and `evidence-report.md` says "Strategy: fitted_markov_balanced".
  - The inclusion count is then approximate while the report implies it is exact.
- **Why it matters:** the "invocation of the selected strategy" and "evidence truth" requirements are not met on this path. Model-planned runs are less exposed because the wire schema requires `strategy` when a spec strategy exists.
- **Minimal rework (either one):**
  - In the hylogger `plan` step, set `plan["strategy"] = spec strategy` when the plan omits it, so the stored plan, the generator and the report agree. Or make `generate` fail when the plan has no strategy and the spec's strategy is not the default.
  - Also fix the docstring at `plan.py:46-47,92`.
- **Test to add:** a spec with `strategy=fitted_markov_balanced` and a plan without `strategy`. Assert the output has the balanced exact valid count and the report names the strategy that ran.
