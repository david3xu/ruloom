## Remaining limits

- Interpretation can still be wrong. Review the proposed plan and evidence.
  Arbitrary fields, custom tolerances and privacy promises are unsupported.
- Balanced counts follow rounded aggregate validity. Source row order, exact
  run-length and spacing distributions, spectra, coordinates and photographs
  are not reproduced by this scalar route.
- AGY deny settings and a no-tools prompt are requested guidance. Unexpected
  tool use is rejected after the call; true process/tool confinement remains
  planned. Use trusted local profiles and do not send private raw rows.
- Tiny toy inputs can fail with an unhelpful index error. Legacy standalone
  repair/evaluation helpers do not measure the new exact-count condition;
  the supported CLI lifecycle does.
- Carried low advisories: explicit GPT effort ergonomics, buffered output and
  source-hash reads, model text in refusal messages and standalone smoke-script
  test coverage. No general model-superiority result was established.
- `request.json` hashes the base interpretation prompt. AGY adds its tool-free
  prefix; the full sent text is retained in invocation argv. These digests
  and transport evidence must not be confused with attested backend weights.
